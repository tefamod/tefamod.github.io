AIPT — AI Personal Trainer

Backend for an AI fitness-coaching app. Turns a food photo or a product barcode into a full nutrition breakdown (calories, protein, fat, sugar…), using a rotating pool of Gemini API keys that auto-cools down on rate limits, plus Open Food Facts for barcode lookups.
